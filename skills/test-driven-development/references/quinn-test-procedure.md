# Quinn — Test Report Delivery Procedure

Loaded on demand by `agents/quinn.md` § 6 when writing the test report or `acceptance-results.md`. This file is the *how*; the persona keeps the scope and the judgement. The grammar of every machine-parsed line — the Coverage Ledger form and its status tokens, what counts as a checkable evidence half, *latest mention wins*, fence blanking, and the acceptance-results shape — is owned by the scripts' own `--help` (`tool_registry.py show check_coverage`, `tool_registry.py show check_acceptance_suite`), with fence blanking shared across the family in `{PLUGIN_ROOT}/pipeline-tools/SKILL.md` § Fenced blocks and encoding. Never restate it here or in the report, and never invent a variant.

- **Header Append**: append under a `#Task [N]:` header — **exactly one header per literal task number**, never a combined or ranged header (`#Task 1/2/3:`, `#Task [1]-[3]:`), because the shape gate and the eval grader match a single integer and read a merged header as a missing task. The header is human-readable only; the gates parse the ledger lines below it.
- **Retests append, never edit**: a retest is a NEW `#Task [N]: (retest)` block at the END of the file, never an in-place edit of the original block — latest-mention-wins means an insertion can be overridden by a stale later line.
- **Retests: say whether the builder's self-check agreed with yours.** A rejection-round builder handoff carries a `<fix_verification>` element, and may cite a capture under `evidence/build/`. When it does, state in the retest block whether your independent run **agreed** with it. A disagreement — the builder's green against your red on the same check — is not noise to overwrite silently: it is the signature of a stale process, a wrong base URL, or a build marker that predates the fix (`{PLUGIN_ROOT}/runtime-evidence/SKILL.md`, *The Capture Artifact*). Name it, and put it in your `<handoff>`. This grants `evidence/build/` no authority — your capture remains the only gating one.
- **Append a status line only for a check you actually ran this round** (`base-persona.md` § Evidence Integrity — never record what you did not observe); if you did not run it, append nothing.
- **Writing `BLOCKED`**: name what was missing and what you observed instead. It counts as **not covered** and the gate exits 1 — honesty routes the work, it does not pass it. Per `base-persona.md`'s Evidence Integrity rules it also belongs in your `<handoff>` as a `blocked_on: environment — <what was missing>` line in `<blockers>`, so it reaches the blockers ledger.
- **Runtime evidence citation**: for any block containing a claim about behavior a client, person, or device can observe, emit a `**Runtime evidence:**` line citing every capture that backs it — success *and* failure paths. The grammar and the capture contract are owned by `{PLUGIN_ROOT}/runtime-evidence/SKILL.md`; do not restate them, and do not invent a variant. The Orchestrator's gate reads these citations, so a claim with no citation is unproven regardless of what the ledger line says.
- **Standing Obligation**: the Coverage Ledger is a standing obligation — a delegation brief narrows scope but never relaxes a mandatory output format. When a brief asks only for a looser narrative, emit both the narrative and the ledger lines.
